# Printing

The STL files are already turned to the print orientation. Load them and slice. No supports are needed.

| Part | Orientation | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Base | Bottom face on the bed. | The keyholes and screw recesses open downward, and the standoffs grow straight up. |
| Lid | Top face on the bed. | The logo and the top slots are on the first layer, and the open rim is the last layer. The board name inside the lid is in the last layer of the ceiling. The fan clips grow straight up, and their 0.8 mm hooks print as small overhangs. |

## Suggested settings

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Material | PETG or ASA. The power supply warms the inside of the box, and PLA can soften near it. |
| Layer height | 0.2 mm |
| Walls | 3 perimeters |
| Infill | 20 % |
| Supports | None |

The logo is recessed 0.2 mm into the lid top, which is one first layer. To print it in a second colour, add a filament change after the first layer.

## Heat-set inserts

Press the inserts in with a soldering iron and an insert tip at about 220 °C for PETG.

- 4 × M2.5 into the Pi standoffs of the base. The holes are tapered by 1° per side, so the insert starts easily and seats tight. Push it flush with the top of the standoff. The hole is deeper than the insert, so the plastic it pushes down has room.
- 4 × M3 into the corner bosses of the lid.
